# 🍱 FoodShare — Food Waste Management & Redistribution Platform

> **Reduce Food Waste. Share Food. Create Impact. 🌱**

FoodShare is a web-based food redistribution platform designed to help reduce food wastage by connecting people or organizations with surplus food to people/organizations who need it.

The platform provides a digital space where surplus food can be listed, discovered, requested, and managed efficiently.

---

## 🌍 Why FoodShare?

Every day, a large amount of perfectly usable food is wasted while many people struggle to access sufficient food.

FoodShare aims to bridge this gap through technology.

Instead of letting surplus food go to waste:

**Donor → FoodShare → Receiver**

The goal is simple:

> **Save food that can still be used and connect it with someone who needs it.**

## ✨ Key Features

### 👤 User Authentication
- Secure user registration and login
- Role-based user experience
- User account management
- Authentication-based access to platform features

### 🍱 Food Donation

Donors can share information about available surplus food.

Users can provide details such as:

- Food name
- Food category
- Quantity
- Location
- Availability
- Additional information

### 🔎 Food Discovery

Receivers can discover available food donations and find suitable listings.

### 📍 Location-Based Food Sharing

Food listings can be associated with locations to make local food redistribution easier.

### 📋 Donation Management

Users can manage their food listings and track the status of their donations.

### 🤝 Donor & Receiver Connection

FoodShare helps create a connection between:

- Restaurants
- Food businesses
- Individuals
- NGOs
- Food recipients
- Community organizations

### 📊 Impact Tracking

The platform can be extended to track:

- Meals donated
- Food rescued
- Active donors
- Successful distributions
- Community impact

---

## 🧑‍💻 User Roles

FoodShare can support multiple types of users.

| Role | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| 🍽️ Donor | List surplus food |
| 🤝 Receiver | Find/request available food |
| 🛠️ Admin | Manage users, donations and platform activity |
